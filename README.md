# 📊 HR Workforce & Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

An HR analytics project focused on analyzing employee attrition patterns and identifying workforce factors associated with employee turnover. The project transforms HR data into meaningful insights through data analysis, SQL queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze workforce composition and employee attrition.
* Identify attrition patterns across different employee groups.
* Examine relationships between attrition, income, overtime, department, and other workforce characteristics.
* Develop an interactive dashboard to communicate key HR metrics and insights.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators

* **Total Employees:** 1,470
* **Active Employees:** 1,233
* **Employee Attrition:** 237
* **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
* **Average Monthly Income:** 6.5K
* **Average Employee Age:** 36.92 years

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Employees working overtime recorded 127 attrition cases, compared with 110 among non-overtime employees.
* Employees earning below 10K accounted for 212 attrition cases, compared with 25 among employees earning 10K or above.
* Employees living closer to the workplace showed higher observed attrition in the dataset.
* Research & Development recorded the highest number of attrition cases.
* Employees who travel rarely for business showed higher observed attrition.
* Male employees recorded 150 attrition cases, compared with approximately 87 among female employees.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python:** Data preprocessing, cleaning, analysis, and visualization
* **Pandas:** Data manipulation and analysis
* **Matplotlib:** Data visualization
* **SQL (MySQL):** Database querying and business analysis
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development
* **DAX:** KPI calculations and analytical measures
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data handling and supporting analysis

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents workforce KPIs and employee attrition patterns through interactive visualizations, enabling users to explore key HR metrics and compare employee groups.

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis highlights workforce segments with notable attrition patterns and identifies areas for further investigation. These insights can support data-driven HR decisions and help inform employee-retention strategies.

## ⚠️ Analytical Limitations

The findings represent patterns and associations observed in the dataset and do not establish causation. Further analysis would be required to determine the underlying reasons for employee attrition.
